How we work

Understand first. Build second.

Every business is different, so we don't begin with assumptions or generic solutions. We start by understanding your business, then build a practical roadmap.

1

Discovery call

A free conversation to understand your business, requirements and challenges, followed by a tailored approach.

2

Diagnose

Understand the business before recommending solutions. We evaluate your processes, sales activities, team structure, tools and performance to find challenges and opportunities.

3

Design

Create a strategy built around your objectives. A practical roadmap covering priorities, processes, targets, tools and implementation requirements.

4

Build

Turn the strategy into working systems. We help implement processes, CRM systems, sales frameworks and team training.

5

Execute & improve

Measure progress and keep improving. We monitor performance, review results and find opportunities to improve processes and outcomes.

Frequently asked questions.

What does Barchs – The Business Architects do?

Barchs is a business consultancy that helps startups, SMEs and established businesses address challenges through consulting, sales strategy, customer acquisition, operational improvements, technology and managed business solutions.

Which businesses can work with Barchs?

Businesses at different stages of growth, including startups, small and medium enterprises, established businesses and service-based organizations, plus specific requirements in sectors such as education and real estate.

What types of consulting services do you provide?

Business strategy, growth planning, sales consulting, sales process optimization, customer acquisition, CRM implementation, business operations and sales team development.

Does Barchs only provide consulting, or also implementation?

Our engagement model goes beyond strategic recommendations. Depending on the project, we support implementation, training and managed execution.

How does the consulting process begin?

With an initial discovery conversation to understand your business, requirements and challenges. We then assess them and propose an appropriate approach.

How much do your consulting services cost?

Pricing depends on scope, requirements, complexity and duration. We provide a tailored proposal after understanding your requirements.

Can businesses hire Barchs for individual services?

Yes. Explore individual services or broader engagements depending on your requirements.

How can I get started with Barchs?

Contact our team through the website or schedule a discovery call to discuss your business requirements.
