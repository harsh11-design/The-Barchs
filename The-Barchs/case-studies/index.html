    Real Estate & Property +310% Pipeline Conversion

    Metropolitan Property Group: Solving Lead Leakage & Automating Multi-Agent Follow-Up

    Client Challenge: The agency spent over $25,000 monthly on Meta and Google property ads, generating 400+ inquiries. However, agents took 24–48 hours to reply manually, spreadsheets were out of date, and 72% of leads were never followed up more than once.

    Barchs Architecture Strategy: We implemented a centralized CRM with instant WhatsApp and SMS multi-touch automation. Incoming buyer inquiries were enriched, scored, and instantly routed to on-duty agents with pre-configured viewing booking links.

    First Response Time:
    From 28 hrs to 4 mins
    Showroom Viewings:
    +310% Increase
    Attributed Sales Volume:
    $14.2M in 6 Months
    "Before Barchs, our agents were drowning in unorganized Excel sheets and losing sales every weekend. The automated pipeline changed our entire commercial momentum within 30 days." — Managing Director, Metropolitan Property Group

    B2B SaaS & Tech $1.2M Outbound ARR

    LogiSync Enterprise: Building an Outbound Sales Machine from Zero to $1.2M ARR

    Client Challenge: A fast-growing supply chain software company had product-market fit with pilot clients but zero structured outbound sales capability. The technical founders were handling all sales calls haphazardly without a repeatable playbook.

    Barchs Architecture Strategy: Barchs architected their complete commercial operating model: ideal customer profile (ICP) criteria, outbound email and LinkedIn cadences, discovery call playbooks, objection scripts, and commission compensation plans. We recruited and coached their first 3 BDEs.

    Qualified Demos/Month:
    From 4 to 38 monthly
    Sales Cycle Length:
    Reduced from 75 to 42 days
    Annual Contract Value:
    Average $32,000/deal
    "Barchs did not just give us slide decks; they built the scripts, trained the reps, and structured our weekly sales pipeline discipline. They are genuine business architects." — CEO & Co-Founder, LogiSync

    Educational Institutions +44% Enrollment Rate

    Horizon Academy Network: Streamlining Multi-Campus Admissions & Telesales

    Barchs Architecture Strategy: Deployed a centralized cloud tele-counseling infrastructure with smart IVR routing, automated application status SMS reminders, and structured script playbooks for admissions counselors.

    Admissions Enrollment:
    +44% YoY Growth
    Counselor Call Output:
    2.4x Daily Completed Calls
    Lead-to-Campus Visit:
    Lifted from 16% to 39%
